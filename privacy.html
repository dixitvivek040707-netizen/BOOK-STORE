Privacy policy.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This is a starter privacy policy for the Enginuity Books demo application. Review and adapt it to your real business practices and applicable law before launching a public store.

Information we collect

When you place a demo order, the app stores the name, email address, delivery address, selected books, quantities, total, and order timestamp you submit. The information is stored in the server's local JSON data file.

How information is used

Order details are used to create and retrieve order records. The demo does not process payments, use advertising trackers, or intentionally sell personal information.

Retention and security

Order records remain in the local data file until the operator removes them. This starter implementation does not include account login, encryption at rest, automated deletion, or production-grade access controls. Do not enter sensitive information.

Third-party services

The storefront loads web fonts from Google Fonts. The hosting provider may collect technical logs under its own policies. Before launch, document every third-party service you actually use.

Your choices and contact

For requests about an order or personal information, contact the store operator using the contact address published by your real business. Replace this placeholder policy with a legally reviewed version and a real contact before launch.
